If your GHL texts land in spam or get blocked, it's almost always A2P 10DLC. Here's the fix.

## What it is

US carriers require businesses to register their **brand** (who you are) and **campaign** (what you send) before bulk SMS. Unregistered traffic gets filtered or fined.

## The registration path in GHL

1. Agency dashboard → Phone Numbers / Trust Center → start Brand registration (EIN + business address must match exactly).
2. Create the Campaign: use-case "Marketing" or "Mixed", sample messages that match what you actually send.
3. Opt-in proof: screenshot the form/checkbox where contacts consent ("By submitting, you agree to receive texts…"). This is where most rejections happen.
4. Wait 3–7 days. Don't send bulk SMS meanwhile.

## Fixing rejections

- Mismatched EIN/address → re-file with exact IRS records.
- Weak opt-in → add explicit SMS consent checkbox, re-screenshot.
- Message mismatch → your samples must mirror real sends, including opt-out language ("Reply STOP to opt out").

Do this once per client brand. Bake it into your onboarding snapshot checklist.
