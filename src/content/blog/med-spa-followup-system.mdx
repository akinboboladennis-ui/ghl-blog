Most med spa leads don't say no. They just never hear back fast enough, often enough. Here's the follow-up system I built that took one spa's lead-to-booking rate from 10% to 40%.

## The problem

Leads from Meta ads sat for hours. No instant response, one reminder call, then silence. Consults went to whoever replied first — usually a competitor.

## The system

1. **Speed-to-lead (0–2 min):** missed-call textback + instant SMS with first name + booking link.
2. **Nurture (days 1–14):** SMS/email sequence — social proof, offer, FAQ handling, second booking push.
3. **No-show recovery:** 24h + 2h reminders, no-show → rescue sequence with rebook incentive.
4. **Review engine:** 2h post-visit Google-review request; 5-stars → public review, anything less → private feedback to staff.

## The result

Lead-to-booking: **10% → 40%**. Same ad spend, 4x consults. Reviews compounded into Maps rankings.
