Before you add AI or fancy dashboards, switch these seven on. They stop 90% of lead leakage.

1. **Instant new-lead SMS** — under 2 minutes, every time, including after hours (with business-hours wording).
2. **Missed-call textback** — every missed call gets an SMS in seconds.
3. **Booking confirmations + reminders** — 24h and 2h, SMS + email. Kills no-shows.
4. **No-show rescue** — automatic rebook sequence the moment a consult is marked no-show.
5. **Long-term nurture** — 30/60/90-day check-ins for "not now" leads. This alone resurrects 10–20% of dead pipelines.
6. **Review requests** — timed SMS after a visit/completion, routed by sentiment.
7. **Dead-opportunity reactivation** — quarterly blast to lost deals with a fresh offer.

Each one is a single workflow. Build them once, snapshot them, deploy to every client.
