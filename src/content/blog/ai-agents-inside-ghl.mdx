An AI agent is only as good as the system it sits on. Here's how I scope agents that actually help a business — drawn from real builds (AI receptionist for a home-services company, CloseBot AI for orthodontic clinics, GHL chatbot for a law firm).

## What AI agents should do in your CRM

- **Answer instantly:** reply to new leads in under 60 seconds on SMS, chat, or voice.
- **Qualify:** ask 3–5 questions (need, timeline, budget, location) and write answers to custom fields.
- **Book:** offer calendar slots and confirm into the right calendar.
- **Update the CRM:** tag, set pipeline stage, notify the human owner with a summary.

## What they shouldn't do

- Quote prices you haven't approved, give medical/legal advice, or argue with angry callers — escalate to a human.
- Run without a knowledge base: connect FAQs, services, pricing bounds first.
- Operate without logging: every conversation should land on the contact timeline.
