Most GHL pipelines are junk drawers — 14 stages nobody updates. Here's the system I install for clients.

## The 5-stage rule

No pipeline gets more than 5–6 stages. Example for a home-services client:

1. New Lead → 2. Contacted → 3. Quote Sent → 4. Won → 5. Lost (with lost-reason required)

Fewer stages = honest reporting. Every stage needs an **entry trigger** (workflow moves it, not humans) and an **exit automation**.

## Automate stage movement

- Form/booking submit → create opportunity in "New Lead" + notify owner.
- No reply in 24h → stay, but fire the nurture workflow.
- Quote sent (email opened/clicked or manual) → move to "Quote Sent".
- Payment via Stripe/order form → "Won" automatically.

Humans forget. Workflows don't.

## Reporting that matters

- Conversion rate per stage (find the leak — it's usually Contacted → Quote).
- Speed-to-lead report (under 5 minutes is the target; GHL's missed-call textback helps).
- Lost-reason breakdown monthly — feed it back into ads and offers.
