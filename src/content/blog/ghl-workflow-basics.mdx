Workflows are where GoHighLevel earns its keep. Here's the minimal lead-nurture automation every account should have.

## The build

1. **Trigger:** Form submitted (or pipeline stage changed).
2. **Action 1:** Send SMS immediately — "Thanks, got your request…".
3. **Wait:** 10 minutes, only during business hours.
4. **Action 2:** Send email with the offer details.
5. **If/else:** Replied? → notify assigned user. No reply in 2 days? → second SMS + voicemail drop.

## Beginner mistakes

- No business-hours guard → 2am texts that kill trust.
- No stop conditions → contacts get nurtured after they already booked.
- Hardcoding phone numbers instead of using custom values.
